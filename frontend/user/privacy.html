At SynapsePM, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal and industrial data when you use our AI-powered predictive maintenance platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account, subscribe to our services, or contact us, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, company name, job title, phone number
  • Authentication Data: Hashed passwords (Argon2id), optional MFA secrets, session metadata (IP address, user agent)
  • Billing Information: Plan selection, billing email, and payment transaction IDs. Full card details are handled by our payment processor (Paddle) and never touch our servers.
  • Communication Preferences: Your notification settings and marketing preferences
  • Support Interactions: Any information you provide when contacting our support team

1.2 Industrial & Equipment Data

To provide our AI-powered predictive maintenance services, we collect and process:

  • Sensor Data: Temperature, vibration, pressure, current, and any other numeric channels you send from your equipment
  • Machine Metadata: Equipment name, type, manufacturer, model, location, installation date, maintenance notes
  • Prediction Records: RUL (Remaining Useful Life) values, risk scores, model versions, and processing times
  • Alerts: Alert titles, severities, and resolution notes generated by the platform
  • Uploaded Datasets: CSV / XLSX / JSON / TXT files you upload for testing or historical analysis

⚠️ Important: Industrial data you upload belongs to you. We process it only to deliver predictions and store it under your organization's tenant boundary.

1.3 Usage & Analytics Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with our platform:

  • Access Logs: IP addresses, login timestamps, browser type, device information
  • Audit Trail: Every create / update / delete action on machines, predictions, alerts, users, and settings
  • Performance Metrics: API response times, prediction latency, error rates
  • Session Data: Pages visited, time spent, navigation patterns

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data powers our AI platform. Specifically, we use it for:

  • Delivering AI Predictions: Running your sensor data through our trained models to compute Remaining Useful Life
  • Real-time Monitoring: Displaying dashboards, generating alerts, and sending email notifications
  • Model Selection: Automatically matching your data to the most compatible trained model family (LSTM, XGBoost, Random Forest, etc.)
  • Account Management: Processing payments, managing subscriptions, and providing customer support
  • Security & Compliance: Detecting unauthorized access, investigating incidents, and complying with legal obligations
  • Platform Improvement: Analyzing aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve performance

Your Industrial Data is Yours — Period.

We never sell your proprietary equipment data. It is used solely to deliver predictions to you, and it is stored with a strict per-organization tenant boundary. You can export or delete your data at any time from your dashboard.

3. Data Security

We implement the following security measures on our platform:

  • Password Hashing: Argon2id (the current industry standard), with automatic PBKDF2 fallback for development only
  • Sensitive Field Encryption: Fernet symmetric encryption for sensitive stored values
  • Transport Encryption: TLS for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers
  • Authentication: JWT access tokens with short expiry, revocable refresh tokens, per-session revocation, and optional TOTP-based multi-factor authentication
  • Tenant Isolation: Every database query is scoped to your organization ID. Cross-tenant access returns 404 — never 403 — so we never confirm that another tenant's data exists
  • Audit Logging: Every sensitive action is recorded in a tamper-evident hash-chained audit log
  • Rate Limiting: Per-user and per-endpoint throttling to prevent abuse
  • Input Validation: All data is sanitized and validated server-side before persistence

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal or industrial data. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted partners that help us operate — our hosting provider, Paddle (for payments), and our transactional email provider. Each is contractually bound to protect your data and cannot use it for other purposes.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with reasonable advance notice
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share your data

5. Your Legal Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete information — you can do this directly from your profile page
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account and personal data from the account settings page. Industrial data associated with your organization is deleted with your machines
  • Right to Data Portability: Export all your data as a ZIP archive (JSON) from the account settings page
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data (e.g., opt out of anonymized analytics)
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: File a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, use your dashboard or contact us at synapsepm121@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies only. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies:

  • Authentication Cookies: Maintain your logged-in session securely
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your dashboard layout, theme, and notification settings
  • Security Cookies: Help detect and prevent fraudulent activity

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this will affect platform functionality (you'll be logged out on every page load).

8. International Data Transfers

SynapsePM is a remote-first platform. Your data is processed on the infrastructure you deploy to — this may be a cloud region you select, or your own on-premise environment. We do not silently move your industrial data between jurisdictions.

If you deploy to a cloud region outside your home jurisdiction and you are an EU resident, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable.

9. GDPR Compliance (For EU / UK Residents)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Our legal bases for processing include: contract performance (providing the service), legitimate interests (security and fraud prevention), legal obligations, and your consent
  • You have the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal
  • You have the right to request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • You have the right to request deletion of your data subject to legal retention requirements
  • You can contact us at synapsepm121@gmail.com for any GDPR-related request

10. CCPA Compliance (For California Residents)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the following rights:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Right to delete personal information (subject to exceptions)
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information — we do not sell any personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise CCPA rights, email synapsepm121@gmail.com.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business and industrial use and are not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

12. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites (documentation, partner pages, blog posts). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes via:

  • Email notification to the address associated with your account (at least 30 days in advance of the change taking effect)
  • In-app notification displayed on your next login
  • A banner on this page for at least 30 days

The "Effective Date" at the top of this policy indicates when the latest version took effect.
