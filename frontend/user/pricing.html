Pricing Questions

Quick answers about plans and billing

swap_horiz

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time from your dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades at the start of the next billing cycle.

verified

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets a 14-day free trial on paid plans. No credit card required. The Free plan is available forever.

credit_card

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay via Paddle. Khalti is also supported for customers in Nepal.

cancel

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your billing page any time. You keep access through the end of the current billing period. No cancellation fees.

discount

Do you offer discounts?

Yes — annual billing saves 20%. We also offer discounts for educational institutions, non-profits, and enterprise volume commitments.

security

How is my data secured?

Passwords hashed with Argon2id, sensitive fields encrypted with Fernet, TLS in transit, per-organization tenant isolation, and full audit logging of every mutation.
