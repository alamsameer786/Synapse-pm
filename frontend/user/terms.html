SynapsePM provides an AI-powered predictive maintenance platform that includes:

  • AI Predictions: Machine learning models (LSTM, Transformer, XGBoost, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, SVM, Neural Network) that analyze sensor data to estimate Remaining Useful Life (RUL) of industrial equipment.
